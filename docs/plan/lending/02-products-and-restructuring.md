# Loan products and editable financing structures

An applicant should be able to request SBA 7(a), conventional business financing or equipment financing without being locked into that choice. The lender should be able to change the total amount and create any supported combination of facilities within the same application. Preserve what the applicant originally asked for, what the bank proposed, what was approved, what the applicant accepted, and what was funded as separate versions.

This is the October 12, 2026 target design. It supersedes the October 7 fixed-product decision, the amount-only approval contract and the one-account-per-application assumption for new structured applications. It does not retroactively change historical decisions or funding records.

## Product catalog

The 19 entries below include **product families and meaningful variants**, not 19 unrelated legal programs. For example, SBA Express is a delivery method within 7(a); secured/unsecured are attributes of conventional lending. Acquisition and refinancing are purposes that can apply to several products. Syndication is a funding/ownership arrangement, not an additional borrower purpose.

“Initial” means proposed for the first implementation, not currently available. Reference entries may appear in a lender research catalog, but must not be presented as supported executable workflows.

Do not assign universal market limits, interest rates or approval times to conventional products. A bank's configured product version owns its terms and limits. SBA subprogram rules need their own dated versions; “SBA” is not a sufficient product identifier.

## The first three product families

Implement a shared origination workflow with these product overlays. These are **proposed demo requirements**, not a complete SBA SOP or a bank credit policy.

| Family | Additional information and analysis | Approval and closing evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SBA 7(a) term | Requested delivery method; eligibility questionnaire; ownership/affiliates and existing SBA exposure; allocated uses; repayment analysis; documented credit-elsewhere assessment; required equity, collateral and guarantees under the selected policy | Staff eligibility review, synthetic program authorization, approved terms, applicable guarantee/security documents and evidence of required contribution |
| Conventional business term | Secured/unsecured selection, purpose, total debt capacity, existing exposure and proposed terms | Bank credit approval; collateral/guarantee evidence when applicable; accepted offer and executed documents |
| Equipment term | Equipment/vendor/quote, purchase price, contribution, proposed asset value and useful life | Credit approval, asset/security evidence, applicable insurance and verified synthetic vendor funding instructions |

A $4 million conventional companion facility must be included in the SBA repayment analysis and combined sources/uses. The companion is not automatically eligible simply because it covers the amount above an SBA limit. Keep program-level constraints separate from bank preferences and ordinary waivable documentation tasks.

## The nine million dollar example

The SBA's current overview caps an individual 7(a) loan at **$5 million**. Preserve a borrower's $9 million total need and SBA preference as a valid request for the bank to structure; flag that $9 million cannot become one eligible 7(a) facility. Do not cap or discard the overall request. [SBA 7(a) overview][sba-7a]

| Version | Borrower need | Proposed financing | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original request | $9,000,000 | Preference: SBA 7(a) | Saved; requires structuring; no approval implied |
| Structure A | $9,000,000 | $5,000,000 SBA 7(a) + $4,000,000 conventional term | Totals reconcile; eligibility, repayment, collateral and authority still require review |
| Structure B | $9,000,000 | $5,000,000 SBA 7(a) + $3,000,000 conventional term + $1,000,000 equipment term | Three facilities; requires compatible uses, collateral and combined repayment analysis |
| Structure C | Revised need: $8,000,000 | $4,500,000 SBA 7(a) + $3,500,000 conventional term | Records why need changed; keeps the original $9 million request in history |
| Unbalanced draft | $9,000,000 | $5,000,000 SBA 7(a) + $3,000,000 conventional term | Saveable proposal with $1 million gap; cannot publish as fully financed without an explicit change or other source |
| Invalid program allocation | $9,000,000 | $6,000,000 SBA 7(a) + $3,000,000 conventional term | Draft warning; program cap blocks credit approval and funding |

The SBA's July 4, 2026 policy change also allows qualifying sequential 7(a) and 504 arrangements under separate program limits. The notice specifically describes approving 7(a) first and the CDC's 504 transaction second; 504 limits include affiliates and differ for qualifying manufacturer/energy projects. This does **not** create a $10 million single 7(a) loan. Keep gross loan principal, SBA-guaranteed exposure, 504 debenture amount, bank companion debt and total project cost distinct. Do not encode either “all SBA financing is capped at $5 million” or “every borrower can combine $10 million.” [SBA Policy Notice 5000-879058, pages 1–2][sba-coordination]

A 504 structure needs a CDC and senior-lender component plus borrower contribution; its debenture limit is not the total project-size limit. It cannot be substituted for ordinary working capital. Reserve that more complex structure for a later product slice. [SBA 504][sba-504]

## Borrower and lender experience

### Product choice during setup

Add one logical setup question: **“What type of financing are you considering?”** Offer the three supported families and “Help me choose.” Record a preference, not a credit decision. Keep the total-amount question independent of one facility's cap. Present useful product guidance; a mismatch creates a lender structuring action rather than silently reducing the request.

Preserve email-first entry, server-saved progress, one question per screen and explicit Finish setup. Staff prefills still require applicant confirmation. Upgrade unfinished setup with a versioned step mapping. Completed applications stay in the portal; collect a missing preference there without restarting setup.

### Financing structure in the lender console

Add a **Financing** workspace with the requested need, project uses, borrower/other contributions, financing gap and a list of facilities. Each facility shows product, amount, terms, allocated uses, collateral/guarantees, current approval and closing/funding state.

Support add, remove, duplicate as draft, split, combine, change product, and change amount. Do not hard-code two facilities or an SBA-plus-conventional pairing. Combining facilities creates an explicit successor; it must not merge executed agreements or historical funding records. Allow incomplete drafts so the lender can explore a structure, while clearly listing blockers before publication, approval or funding.

Provide a before/after comparison and a required reason for publishing a revision. Show affected evidence, approvals, offers, signatures and funding holds. Unchanged document evidence can be reused only where the same current source and access scope still satisfy the new requirement. A change preview itself has no side effects.

Borrowers see the published proposal and their next actions, not staff-only drafts or committee notes. They may revise their preference/need during editable collection; during review or closing they request a change that the bank processes through a new revision. Acceptance names the complete version and its facilities.

## Proposed domain model

Use the existing `Application` as the financing-request container. Do not create a new unrelated application for every facility or reset participants/setup when a product changes.

| Record | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| ProductDefinition and ProductPolicyVersion | Stable family/variant, support status, bank configuration, sourced constraints, effective/review dates and rules |
| FinancingRequestRevision | Original/current requested need, product preference, purpose and applicant confirmation |
| FinancingStructureRevision | Immutable published package; mutable draft predecessor; revision reason, author, source request and active facility membership |
| Facility and FacilityVersion | Stable identity and lineage for each proposed credit; exact principal/commitment, type, terms, uses, collateral and program policy references |
| UseOfFunds and FundingSourceAllocation | Project costs, contributions and allocations; no double financing of the same expense |
| UnderwritingPackageRevision | Shared business analysis plus facility analysis, combined obligations, evidence and policy fingerprints |
| ApprovalCase and ApprovalVote | Authority requirements and explicit votes against one package; final decision separate from an analyst recommendation |
| OfferVersion and Acceptance | Customer-visible structure/terms, expiry, response and accepted version |
| FacilityClosingPackage | Facility documents and conditions plus shared package conditions and cross-facility dependencies |
| FundingAuthorization and FundingAttempt | Approved release instructions, preparer/releaser, current inputs and confirmed/failed/uncertain result |
| FacilityFundingRecord and LoanAccount | One initial confirmed funding/account per term facility; application has zero to many accounts |

These names are design guidance, not a requirement to create all tables in L01. Preserve normalized bank/application/facility foreign keys and define database uniqueness when the owning task lands.

### Monetary invariants

- Store exact USD decimals. Keep requested financing, proposed commitment, approved commitment, initial disbursement and project cost separate.
- For the initial fully disbursed term products, proposed financing equals the sum of active facility principal. Project uses reconcile to financing plus explicitly identified borrower/other sources. Fees have a specified payer/funding treatment; do not double-count them as both a cost and a second advance.
- Underfunded or overallocated drafts can be saved with blockers. Publishing an offer requires reconciled uses or an explicitly defined reduction in the project's scope. The borrower must accept that reduced scope before closing; never invent equity to fill a gap or fund an unexplained shortfall.
- Check facility-specific limits, shared program exposure and related-borrower exposure. Splitting across applications or subprogram labels must not bypass an aggregate constraint. Obtain only authorized same-bank exposure; absent information remains unknown and creates a review task.
- A future line of credit may be established without drawing its full commitment. Keep that distinction in contracts now, but do not enable revolving products using the current exact-approved-amount funding shortcut.

### Restructuring by stage

| Current stage | Allowed change | Required effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft or collecting information | Edit need/preference; staff builds or revises facilities | Version the structure and reconcile affected requirements; preserve setup and evidence |
| Submitted or under review | Staff opens an amendment; applicant supplies requested changes | Hold decisions/funding; preserve submission; create a new review package and obtain necessary applicant confirmation |
| Approved or offer accepted | Staff proposes changed product, amount or split | Preserve prior decision/acceptance as historical; suspend its use for funding; require new package approval and offer acceptance |
| Closing or signing | Same amendment path | Invalidate affected closing clearance; cancel/void outstanding affected envelopes through durable intent; executed artifacts remain historical; re-sign changed documents |
| Partially funded | Revise only unfunded facilities with a successor package | Carry funded facilities forward unchanged; re-underwrite total exposure and remaining funding dependencies; never “unfund” or rewrite booked amounts |
| Fully funded | No in-place origination edit | A future modification/refinance process would create linked records; funded-debt servicing changes remain deferred |

Starting an amendment must be an explicit authorized command that atomically places the affected package on hold. Merely opening a screen does not do so. The old funding endpoint must respect the hold. Abandoning an amendment cannot automatically revive expired, stale or revoked approval; require a deliberate current-evidence check.

For the first implementation, any published material facility change invalidates the **whole package's credit approval and borrower acceptance**, even if only one facility changes. Preserve unaffected evidence; do not claim unaffected approvals remain usable without dependency analysis. Once a facility has funded, its historic approval remains part of that immutable funded record, while the successor decision covers the remaining structure and total exposure.

Race rule: restructuring and funding serialize on the same application/structure boundary. If funding commits first, the amendment must treat that facility as funded. If the amendment/hold commits first, the stale release fails. Duplicate commands and callbacks recover the same result.

### Decisions and aggregate status

An application-wide credit decision records the approved package and each facility's approved terms. Facility closing and funding then progress independently only when the package policy permits it. Store cross-facility conditions such as “both facilities close together” or “fund facility B only after A.” Partial funding must be explicitly allowed, not inferred from the existence of two rows.

Expose a derived facility summary and financing state such as `unstructured`, `proposed`, `in_review`, `approved`, `closing`, `partially_funded`, `funded` or `closed_without_full_funding`. These are proposed contracts, not current enum values. Keep amendment holds, notice status and failed provider attempts separate. “Funded” means all active committed facilities in the accepted package have confirmed initial funding; cancelled/unfunded facilities remain visible in history. Borrower withdrawal after partial funding closes only the remaining request and cannot imply funded debt disappeared.

## Legacy migration and access

Migrate a legacy single-product application to one legacy facility, retaining its actual Synthetic Business Credit version. Never relabel it SBA or claim historical SBA eligibility. Attach existing decisions, closing packages, funding and accounts through additive mapping records; keep original snapshots and identifiers readable.

Existing uniqueness constraints are application-wide in several places. Replace only the constraints whose owning task changes semantics: funding/account uniqueness becomes per facility, and decisions need a reviewed-package/submission revision boundary. Do not simply remove constraints and permit duplicates. Replayed pre-migration commands must still resolve their original result.

Keep full applicants scoped to their application; owner/adviser access still requires explicit task/document grants. A participant on one facility must not gain another facility's private guarantees or personal financial evidence. Shared evidence references remain within the application and must pass current authorization. Bank membership is necessary but insufficient for approval/funding authority once the new controls are enabled.

## Sources and policy maintenance

Accessed October 12, 2026. Primary source links appear beside catalog claims. In addition to those sources, [SBA Policy Notice 5000-879058][sba-coordination] was published May 18, effective July 4, 2026; its PDF displays an August 1, 2027 expiry. Recheck the applicable SOP and later notices before implementing or refreshing a program policy. That notice's sequencing matters; do not replace it with a generic combined-cap number.

Persist source URL, source section, publication/effective date when available, last-reviewed date and policy version. Distinguish a sourced program restriction, a configurable bank policy, and a fixture assumption. A generic staff waiver cannot override a sourced program maximum. Unknown, unsupported or expired policy applicability produces manual review or a block, never an invented eligibility result. No live source fetch runs in the borrower browser or automatically rewrites an active approval.
